Current build

LiverTransformer

A liver-specific transformer pre-trained with masked gene prediction on 1.04M human liver cells from CELLxGENE Census. It transfers well to spatial tasks, including recovering hepatocyte zonation from spatial transcriptomics.

The repository is private for now. I plan to release it after internal validation.

Software

Open-source tools from day-to-day analysis work.

  • spatialzones

    Assigns cells to inside, interface, and outside tumor regions in spatial transcriptomics using nearest-neighbor context. Includes visual diagnostics and downstream expression profiling. Works with Xenium, Visium, and similar platforms. The map at the top of this page walks through the same three steps.

    SpatialPython

  • spatialxgene

    Interactive viewer for spatial transcriptomics stored as h5ad files. Datashader rendering, linked spatial, UMAP, PCA, and scVI views, lasso-to-DGE selection, and stitched multi-slide datasets.

    ViewerPython

  • nextflow-scrna-cloud

    scRNA-seq and Perturb-seq pipeline on Nextflow DSL2 and AWS Batch with Spot instances. Runs from STARsolo alignment through TileDB-SOMA atlas ingestion, with a PyTorch DataLoader for foundation-model training.

    PipelineNextflow

  • sc-ai-hb

    Combines single-cell tumor profiles with LLM reasoning to classify hepatoblastoma subtypes and propose interpretable hypotheses, with supporting literature.

    AI scientistPython

  • scAM.jl

    Single-cell analysis in Julia: preprocessing, clustering, marker discovery, and UMAP, written as a fast alternative to the usual R and Python workflows.

    ToolkitJulia

Research at UCSF

Translational projects in the Wang Lab and with collaborators.

Hepatoblastoma tumor atlas

Single-cell, single-nuclei, and Xenium spatial atlases of pediatric liver cancer, with about 1.8M cells profiled in situ across 17 patients, mapping transcriptional heterogeneity and subtype-specific programs. Tumor-region assignment and DNA/RNA integration feed into subtype calls.

snRNA-seqXeniumMulti-omics

Autoimmune hepatitis

Bulk, single-nuclei, and spatial transcriptomics from patient biopsies, integrated to find candidate autoantigens and immune pathways that could be targeted.
